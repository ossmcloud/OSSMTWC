Glossary

TermMeaning
AccommodationA building or cabin on the site that houses ground and indoor equipment.
ACL (Accredited Contractors List)The contractors a customer allows to work on its behalf.
Agent passAllows a contractor's people to act for a customer: raise SAFs and see that customer's equipment and tickets.
ATMEAdditional Tower Mounted Equipment, attached to a TME.
AzimuthThe compass direction an antenna points to (0–360 degrees).
Designated contactA person nominated by a company for a role, for example signatory, insurance or accreditation contact.
EL / PL / PIEmployer's Liability, Public Liability and Professional Indemnity insurance.
Equipment actionOne piece of work arising from an SRF (licence / install, unlicence / remove, swap), completed through a SAF.
Equipment libraryTowercom's catalogue of approved equipment makes and models.
FeederA cable connecting tower equipment to ground equipment.
GADGeneral Arrangement Drawing.
GIEGround & Indoor Equipment.
MEWPMobile Elevating Work Platform (cherrypicker).
Operator Site IDThe customer's own reference for a site.
PICWPerson In Charge of Works on a site visit.
PortfolioA grouping of Towercom sites. Map pins are coloured by portfolio.
ProfileA person's record in Nexus, linked to a company.
RAMSRisk Assessment and Method Statement.
RadixThe legacy system Nexus replaces. Migrated records are marked "Migrated from Radix".
SAFSite Access Form: a request to access a site.
Safe PassThe basic health & safety certificate everybody needs to go to site.
SDSSite Data Sheet: the licence agreement for a customer's equipment on a site.
SRFSpace Request Form: a request for space on a site to install, remove or swap equipment.
StructureThe mast, tower or other structure equipment is mounted on.
Time blockA bookable part of a day on a site's access calendar.
TLTowercom.
TMETower Mounted Equipment.
Trouble TicketA report of a problem or works needed on a site, linked to a Towercom support case.
Works PermittedThe date from which the works on an executed SRF may be carried out.
