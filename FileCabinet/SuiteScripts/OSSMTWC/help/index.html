Welcome to Nexus

Nexus is the Towercom (TL) portal for sites, equipment, space requests, site access and trouble tickets. It replaces the legacy Radix system.

Towercom owns and manages telecommunication sites: masts, towers, rooftops and the buildings and ground space around them. Customers (mobile operators and other tenants) put equipment on these sites, and contractors go to the sites to install, remove, swap or maintain that equipment. Nexus is where all of this is requested, approved and tracked.

What you can do in Nexus

Find a site

Search sites on a map or in a list and open the site page: location, access instructions, structures, files and activity.

View equipment

See the equipment installed on a site (TME, ATME, GIE, feeders) with its install and licence status.

Request space (SRF)

Ask Towercom for space on a structure to install, remove or swap equipment.

Licences (SDS)

Review, sign and download the Site Data Sheet: the licence agreement produced at the end of an SRF.

Request site access (SAF)

Book time on a site for a survey or works. Nobody may access a site without an approved SAF.

Report a problem

Raise a Trouble Ticket for a site issue, or give safety / security feedback.

Manage your company

Keep your company details, insurance, people and certificates up to date to stay accredited.

Who uses Nexus

UserTypical tasks
Customers (operators / tenants)Find sites, view their equipment, raise Space Requests, sign Site Data Sheets, raise Site Access requests and Trouble Tickets, maintain their company profile.
Contractors and sub-contractorsRaise Site Access requests (for themselves or on behalf of the customers they work for), upload completion photos, maintain their company, people, insurance and certificates.
Towercom staffEverything above, plus reviewing and approving requests, running the SRF workflow, producing and executing licences, assessing tickets, approving documents and maintaining site data.

A company can be a customer, a contractor or both. What you see depends on your company type and on the permissions given to your role. See Permissions.

How the pieces fit together

A typical journey for new equipment on a site:

Find the site→ Space Request (SRF)→ Towercom review→ SRF Approved→ Licence (SDS) issued, signed, executed→ Works Permitted→ Site Access (SAF) for the works→ Completion photos→ Equipment Installed / Removed

Surveys and other visits that don't change equipment only need a Site Access request.
