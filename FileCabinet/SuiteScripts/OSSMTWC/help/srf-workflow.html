SRF Workflow

How the workflow works

When a customer submits an SRF, Nexus creates its workflow: a list of steps (stages), each with a planned date. Steps are set up by Towercom administrators, so the exact steps you see depend on that set-up. Broadly:

Initial Review→ Checks (Space, Facilities, Estates, Accounts)→ TL Response→ SRF Approved (customer accepts)→ SDS / pack produced→ Reviewed & issued→ Customer signs→ TL executes

Completing a step can move the SRF to a new status (shown as the SRF Status Transition on the step). When the customer re-submits after feedback, a new Feedback Loop of steps is added.

Opening the workflow

On the SRF page (any status except Draft):

  • Open Workflow: opens the workflow in a pop-up.
  • View Workflow: opens it in a new tab, with an Open SRF button to go back.

The header shows Site Name, Customer, SRF #, the workflow status and the Works Permitted Date.

The workflow table

Columns: Step, Description, Assigned To, Planned, Actual, Profile, and a pencil (editable) or lock icon.

  • Planned dates turn amber within 2 days of the due date and red when due or overdue.
  • Changing a planned date moves all later steps by the same number of working days.
  • Steps added after a re-submission are grouped under Feedback Loop (n).
Planned dates count working days but don't skip bank holidays. Adjust them by hand if needed.

Working on a step

Click the pencil on a step to open it. The dialog shows:

  • Actual and Planned dates.
  • SRF Status Transition: the SRF status set when you complete this step.
  • Next Steps: for each following step, an Assign to employee and, if the step is optional, a Required toggle.
  • The step's own form: checks, comments, drawings, buttons such as Generate SDS.

Click Confirm to complete the step. A completed step can only be changed again by the person who completed it, if the step allows it and the next TL Response hasn't been completed.

TL Response: approve or give feedback

TL Response is the decision step. It becomes available when all the assigned checks are complete. It shows:

  • a Checks Summary: each check as Passed, Failed or Pending, with who and when;
  • Comments (visible to the customer on the SRF): these become the review comments on the SRF and the default additional conditions on the SDS;
  • a choice to approve or issue feedback.

Approve is only offered when every completed check passed. Issuing feedback sets the SRF to Feedback Issued; the customer edits and re-submits, and a new feedback loop begins.

When approved, the SRF becomes SRF Approved. The customer then accepts it with Accept and Request License, which moves it to License Requested.

Producing the SDS

On the SDS production step, click Generate SDS. Three dialogs follow.

1. SDS Pack Produce Info

  • Commencement Date*
  • Drawing (from the SRF's drawings) and Drawing Reference*
  • Include Licence Map (where the customer uses licence maps)
  • Additional SRF Conditions, Power Supply Comments*
  • Fibre Rights: when on, Fibre Provider, Fibre Duct Route and Notes / Conditions are required
  • Previous Fee, Fee Reduction, Fee Uplift, New Fee, Fee Change Breakdown*
  • Agreement Template* (the customer's active agreements) and Agreement Condition*

2. SDS Agreement Conditions

Fill in the values for each chosen condition.

3. SDS Equipments

Lists all the customer's equipment on the site, not only this SRF's. Tick Include for the equipment the licence covers; ticking a parent ticks its children. You can correct Install Status and Licence Status here. Setting the licence status to Draft or Unlicenced unticks Include.

Click OK to generate the PDF. It opens in a pop-up. If nothing appears, allow pop-ups for the site.

While the SDS is a Draft it is regenerated each time, so you can correct and generate again.

Issuing, signing and executing

  1. Issue: on the review / issue step, click Issue SDS. The SRF becomes License Issued and the customer's signatories can sign.
  2. Reject: Reject SDS with a rejection comment sends the SDS production step back to In Progress.
  3. Sign: the customer signs on the SRF (License Signed). See Signing the SDS.
  4. Execute: employees allowed to execute packs see Execute SDS on the SRF. The dialog shows who signed and when. Clicking OK enters into a legally binding agreement.

On execution: the SRF becomes License Executed, the SDS becomes Current (the previous one Superseded), and the equipment becomes Licenced from the commencement date.

Works Permitted

From License Requested onwards, set the Works Permitted Date in the workflow header (it can't be in the future). The SRF then shows the Works Permitted badge, and its equipment actions can be added to a SAF.

When every equipment action of an executed SRF is done, a nightly job sets the SRF to Completed.

Reviews, equipment actions and other tools

  • Reviews (SRF page): every submission and its result: customer comments, TL result, review date, TL comments, checks, new licence fee and fee breakdown.
  • Equipment Actions (SRF page): every action created by the SRF (Licence, Unlicence, Swap) with its status and the SAF it is on.
  • Lib link on an SRF line: create an Equipment Library entry from the item.
  • Staff with Full permission can Save changes to a submitted SRF.
  • On the NetSuite SRF record: Open SRF opens the portal page.
