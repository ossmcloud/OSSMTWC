Opening your Company Profile

Open Company Profile from the menu. Customers and contractors always see their own company. Click Edit to change the company details (if you have Edit permission).

Managing documents and people (add, edit, delete) needs Full permission and is done in view mode, not edit mode.

Company details

  • Main Information: TL ID, Name, Company Number (read only); Registered Office and Company Address (editable); SAF Auto Approve (read only).
  • Accreditation Info (read only): Status, Submitted, Approved, Commencement, Expiry and Comment. Set by Towercom.

Insurance

The Insurance Info section holds the Insurer and, for each of EL (Employer's Liability), PL (Public Liability) and PI (Professional Indemnity): Status, Available / Type, Limit, Currency and Expiry.

To renew insurance:

  1. Upload the new certificate in the Insurance Files table (see documents).
  2. Towercom checks the certificate and updates the insurance details, expiry and status.

If you change an insurance field yourself, its status goes back to Pending until Towercom checks it. An EL or PL expiry date in the past sets the status to Expired.

A SAF can't be submitted if the customer's or primary contractor's active insurance expires before the last day of the SAF. Renew in good time.

Customers and contractors lists

This section lists the companies you work with. Its name depends on your company type:

  • Customers: Accredited Contractors List: the contractors allowed to work for you.
  • Contractors: Associated Customers List: the customers you work for, plus your Sub Contractors List.

Companies marked "- (Not currently valid for SAF)" are not accredited at the moment and can't be used on a SAF.

H&S and Method Statements

The H&S / Method Statements section holds the Contractor Files: health & safety documents and method statements (RAMS). Contractors attach these to their SAFs in Step 5.

Document tables show: Uploaded, Status, Type, File Name, Rev., Description. Click the preview icon to open a file.

Adding or replacing a document

  1. Click the add icon (or the edit icon on an existing document to upload a new version).
  2. Choose the Type and the file, and add a description.
  3. Save. The document is Pending until Towercom checks it. A new version raises the revision number.

People (profiles)

The Profiles section lists your company's people with their position, designated-contact role, accreditation status, PICW flag and certificate expiry dates. Each certificate date is coloured: Active Expired Pending.

Adding a person

  1. Click the add icon in the profiles table.
  2. Fill in Name, Position, Email and Phone (mandatory), plus Experience and ENSUP Card.
  3. Upload their certificates (see below).
  4. Save. The person starts as Pending until Towercom accredits them.

On existing profiles, name, email, experience and ENSUP card can only be changed by Towercom. Accreditation status, PICW and User Action Needed are set by Towercom.

A small info icon next to a name means the person belongs primarily to another company.

Certificates

Each person can hold these certificates: Safe Pass, Climber, Rescue, Rooftop, RF, Drone, Electrician.

In the profile dialog, each certificate box has View, Upload and History.

  • Upload a PDF of the new certificate. Its status becomes Pending and its expiry is cleared until Towercom checks it. For Safe Pass you must also enter the Safe Pass ID.
  • The old certificate is kept and can be seen in History.
Certificate statusMeaning

Nexus downgrades statuses automatically when certificates and insurance expire. Moving back to Accredited is done by Towercom after checking the new documents.

Expiry reminders

Nexus emails reminders 30 days before and on the day a certificate or insurance expires:

  • Insurance reminders go to your company's designated Insurance contacts.
  • Certificate reminders go to the person and to your company's designated Accreditation contacts.

Make sure Towercom has the right designated contacts for your company.

Staff can open any company. Staff also see each listed company's accreditation status, comments and expiry, and they set people's accreditation status, PICW flag and certificate expiry dates. See approving files.
