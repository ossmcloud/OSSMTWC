Site Data Sheets (SDS)

The licence agreement produced at the end of a Space Request.

The Site Data Sheet is the licence between Towercom and the customer for the equipment on a site. It is a PDF that includes the agreement, the conditions, the fees, the list of licenced equipment, the approved drawing and, where applicable, the licence map and site access documents.

Each customer has one Current SDS per site. When a new SDS is executed, the previous one becomes Superseded.

How an SDS is produced

  1. The customer accepts the approved SRF with Accept and Request License (status License Requested).
  2. Towercom produces the licence pack: commencement date, drawing, conditions, fees and the list of equipment. See SRF Workflow → Producing the SDS.
  3. Towercom reviews and issues it (status License Issued).
  4. An authorised customer signatory signs it (status License Signed).
  5. Towercom executes it (status License Executed). The SDS becomes Current, and the equipment becomes Licenced from the commencement date.

Signing the SDS

Only people in the customer company who are set up as signatories (designated contacts who can sign) see the Sign SDS button. It appears on the SRF when the licence is issued.

  1. Open the SRF and click Sign SDS.
  2. The Sign SDS dialog shows the PDF on the left. Read it.
  3. Read the disclaimer: "By clicking the 'Approve & Sign SDS' button, you (as an authorised representative of the Operator...)..."
  4. Switch on the Approve & Sign SDS toggle and click OK.

If you don't switch on the toggle you get "You need to check the "Approve & Sign SDS" toggle".

Signing the SDS is a legal act on behalf of your company. If you aren't authorised to sign, don't. Ask Towercom to update your company's designated contacts.
If you think the SDS is wrong, don't sign it. Contact Towercom, who can reject the SDS and produce a corrected one.

Viewing and printing

From License Issued onwards, the SRF shows Print SDS, which opens the PDF.

The SDS is only visible to the SRF's own customer company and to Towercom. Contractors acting on behalf of a customer can't see, print or sign it.

SDS Files page

The SDS Files page lists all your company's licences in one place.

  • Filters: Site, Status, Counties and location.
  • Columns: SDS, Site, SRF, Status, County, Commencement Date, Created.
  • Click the eye icon to view the PDF, or the Download icon to save it. The file name is made of site, customer, SDS number and date.

SDS statuses

StatusMeaning
DraftBeing prepared by Towercom, or issued and awaiting signature / execution.
CurrentExecuted: the licence now in force for this customer and site.
SupersededReplaced by a newer executed SDS.
CancelledThe SRF was cancelled before the licence was executed.
