Space Requests (SRF)

Ask Towercom for space on a site to install, remove or swap equipment.

A Space Request Form (SRF) lists the equipment you want to install, remove or swap on one structure of a site. Towercom reviews it, approves it or sends feedback. Once approved, Towercom issues a licence: the Site Data Sheet (SDS). When the licence is executed and works are permitted, the works are done under a Site Access request.

Finding Space Requests

Open Space Request from the menu. Filters: Name, SRF ID, SRF STATUS, Customer, Submitter Company, County and Filter by Location. Click an SRF ID to open it.

The Site page also lists a site's SRFs in Existing SRFs.

Creating a Space Request

An SRF is always for one site. Start it from:

  • the [S.R.F.] link on a map pin, or
  • the New SRF button on the Site page.

You need Edit permission on Space Requests.

The form, step by step

Customer Information

  • Customer: customers get their own company. It can't be changed after the first save.
  • Operator Site ID (mandatory): your own reference for the site. It is filled in automatically when known.
  • Structure: the structure (mast, tower, ...) the equipment goes on. It applies to all TME, ATME and feeder lines. If you change it after adding lines, Nexus asks you to confirm before updating them.

Adding equipment

Before adding a line, pick the Customer, and for TME / ATME / feeders the Structure. Otherwise you get "You need to specify a customer" or "You need to select a structure".

Click the ADD ... button of the right table. The Add new SRF Item dialog has these sections.

Basic Info

  • Request Type: Install, Remove or Swap. It can't be changed once the line is saved.
  • Equipment (Remove and Swap): click ... to pick the existing equipment.
  • TME (ATME / feeders): click ... to pick the existing TME the new item goes on.
  • Item Type, Description.
  • Pick From Library: choose a make and model from the Towercom equipment library.

Equipment Specifications

Make, Model, Length / Width / Depth (mm), Weight (kg), Voltage Type, and for GIE the accommodation and GIE Location. Not used for feeders.

Deployment Details

  • TME / ATME: Height on Tower, Azimuth (0 to 360), B-End, Customer Ref., Modified Antenna Location.
  • Feeders: Structure and Feeder Type.
  • GIE: Customer Ref.

Related Equipment (TME only)

Add the ATME and feeders that belong to the TME directly from its dialog. When swapping a TME, tick the related items you want to swap too.

The equipment library

For some equipment types you must pick from the library: the specification fields stay hidden until you do, and saving without a library item gives "You must pick an item from the library". In the pick item from library dialog, type in the search box, select a row and click add, or just double-click the row.

Picking existing equipment (Remove / Swap)

The pick item from list dialog lists your Licenced equipment on the site (on the SRF's structure for TME, ATME and feeders). Double-click a row to pick it. The Note column warns about problems:

  • Red, cannot be picked: "already selected for removal / swap on SRF-..." or "there is an outstanding SAF (...) against this equipment, please contact TL".
  • Amber, can be picked after confirming: "this equipment was never installed".

Swaps

A swap shows the old values struck through and the new values in bold. The new equipment shows as TBA until the SRF is submitted, then gets its own equipment number.

Use add and duplicate to add a line and start a copy of it straight away. It is handy for several identical antennas. Only available for Install.

Saving, submitting and cancelling

In edit mode the buttons at the bottom of the form depend on the status:

StatusButtons
New or DraftSave As Draft, Submit SRF, Cancel SRF
Feedback IssuedSave, Re-Submit SRF
  • Save As Draft keeps your work without sending it to Towercom. You can come back and finish it later.
  • Submit SRF sends it for review. You must enter a comment ("Please enter a comment for this submission"). An SRF needs at least one equipment line and one attached file.
  • Re-Submit SRF, after feedback, asks for a comment describing the changes you made.
  • Cancel SRF asks for a reason. The reason is recorded in Submission Notes.

Saving can take a moment; the progress message shows what is being saved (files, items, ...). If some parts fail, they are listed under "Some error occurred while saving:".

After you submit

  • Towercom reviews the request (space, facilities, estates and accounts checks).
  • If changes are needed, the SRF moves to Feedback Issued. A red Towercom Feedback Info banner at the top shows the review date and comments. Click Edit & Re-Submit, make the changes and re-submit.
  • If approved, the SRF moves to SRF Approved and a green Towercom Approval Info banner shows the comments and, where relevant, the New License Fee, Fee Uplift or Fee Reduction. Click Accept and Request License to continue.
  • Towercom then produces the licence (SDS) for you to sign. See Site Data Sheets.
  • Once Towercom executes the licence and records the Works Permitted date, a dark-green Works Permitted badge appears on the SRF. You can now raise the Site Access request for the works.
  • When all the equipment on the SRF has been installed or removed through Site Access, the SRF becomes Completed automatically.
SRFs migrated from Radix show a magenta Migrated from Radix badge.

Who can do what

  • Customers see all SRFs of their company. They can edit an SRF while it is a Draft, or after Feedback Issued.
  • Contractors acting for a customer (agent pass) see the SRFs their own company raised for that customer.
  • Fee details are shown to customers from SRF Approved onwards.
