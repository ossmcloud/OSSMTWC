Site Access (SAF)

Book access to a site for a survey, installation, removal, swap or any other work.

A site cannot be accessed without an approved Site Access Form (SAF). Make sure your SAF shows Approved before anyone goes to site.

Before you start

  • Your company must be Accredited (or To Be Renewed). Otherwise you see "Your accreditation status [...] does not allow for this action". See Accreditation.
  • Everybody going to site must have a profile in Nexus, be Accredited, and hold an Active Safe Pass plus the certificates the work needs. All certificates must be valid until the last day of the SAF.
  • The customer's and the primary contractor's EL / PL / PI insurance must be valid until the last day of the SAF.
  • For installs, removals and swaps, the SRF must have a Works Permitted date. See Space Requests.

Finding Site Access requests

Open Site Access from the menu. Filters: Site, SAF ID, Status, Access Type, Customer, Contractor, Counties, Start Date, End Date.

The list shows the SAF ID, site, type, submitted date, author, customer, primary contractor, PICW, status, start and end, and the access flags (mast, building, rooftop, crane, electrical, drone). Click the SAF ID to open it, or re-use to start a new SAF from it.

Which SAFs you see:

  • Customers: all SAFs for their company.
  • Contractors: SAFs where their company is the primary contractor, and SAFs that colleagues with the same agent pass raised for a customer.

Creating a SAF

Start a SAF from:

  • the [S.A.F.] link on a map pin,
  • the New SAF button on the Site page, or
  • re-use on an existing SAF (see Re-using a SAF).

If the site doesn't allow any type of access for you, the page shows CANNOT PROCEED: "This site does not allow Access of any type".

Choosing dates and time blocks

The top of the form, Create New Site Access, is a calendar. Each day is coloured:

  • Green: free.
  • Amber: some time blocks are already booked.
  • Red: fully booked.

Click a day to see its time blocks:

The Conditions of Access panel on the right keeps a running summary:

  • the time-block limit for the SAF type and how many you've allocated (red if you have too many);
  • each reserved block, with a bin icon to remove it;
  • the crew requirements, for example 2 Climber Required, Of Which 1 Rescue Climber Required, All Crew Rooftop Certified Required;
  • "This request will be automatically approved" when it qualifies for auto-approval;
  • red warnings when the chosen structure or accommodation doesn't allow this SAF type.

The five steps

Step 1 of 5 : SAF SETUP

  • Template: Create new SAF or Reuse previous SAF. If re-using, pick the S.A.F. to copy: the new SAF is for the site you are on.
  • You can also re-use a SAF with the re-use link in the SAF list or the Re Use button on a SAF: keep the original site or pick a different one.
  • Re-use is refused if the customer or primary contractor is not Accredited / ACA to be Renewed. Type, customer, contractor, summary of work and documents (still valid) are copied; time blocks are not. The PICW and crew members are copied only if still accredited (the ones dropped are listed in Step 4). On the same site all access answers, structure and accommodation are copied; on a different site only Electrical Work and Drone Use are kept, the other answers must be entered again.
  • Type: the type of access (for example survey, or installation works). The type sets the time-block limit and whether equipment actions are needed.

Step 2 of 5 : Access Requirements

Answer Yes or No to each question the site asks:

  • Mast Access, then pick the Structure.
  • Climb Over 60m (only for structures over 60 m).
  • TL Building Access, then pick the Accommodation.
  • Rooftop Access. On rooftop sites, mast access also means rooftop access.
  • Electrical Work
  • Crane / Cherrypicker
  • Drone Use

Steps 3 to 5 and the Submit button appear once every question is answered.

Step 3 of 5 : Access Details

  • Customer: customers get their own company.
  • Primary Contractor: the company responsible for the works. Contractors get their own company. The customer itself can also be the primary contractor.
  • Summary of Work: describe what will be done.
  • Equipment Actions: only for SAF types linked to SRF works (installs, removals, swaps). See below.

Adding equipment actions

  1. Click ADD EQUIPMENT ACTIONS.
  2. In the manage action dialog, pick the S.R.F. Only SRFs for this site and customer with a Works Permitted date and pending actions are listed.
  3. Tick the actions to carry out on this visit. Ticking a TME also ticks its ATME and feeders.

Actions already on another SAF show detach from <SAF>. You can move them to this SAF by giving a Detach Reason (and comment where required). Completed actions can't be picked.

Step 4 of 5 : Crews Visitors

  • PICW (Person In Charge of Works): pick from the primary contractor's people who are approved as PICW and hold a valid Safe Pass. Their phone and certificates are shown.
  • Click ADD CREW MEMBER to add each other person: pick the Contractor and the Person. Their valid certificates are shown (for example Climber [2027-01-01]). The PICW can't also be in the crew list.

Only people who are Accredited and have an Active Safe Pass can be picked.

Step 5 of 5 : Documentation

The primary contractor's Health & Safety and Method Statement documents (RAMS) are listed by type. Switch on the toggle for each document that applies to this visit. Click the download icon to preview a document.

If the list says "no documentation available", the contractor has no received documents yet. Upload them on the Company Profile and wait for Towercom to accept them.

Submitting

Click Submit and confirm. If something is missing, Nexus shows "The SAF cannot be saved:" and a list, for example:

  • Specify at least one time block / Too many time-blocks allocated
  • Specify a customer / Specify a primary contractor / Specify a summary of work comment
  • Specify a PICW / Specify a structure / Specify an accommodation
  • Specify at least one action
  • Certificate problems (see above)
  • Insurance problems, for example "Company: EL Insurance will be expired by ...". If the problem is with another company, the message asks you to contact them.

Approval

Some SAFs are approved automatically: the Conditions of Access panel says "This request will be automatically approved". That needs:

  • a SAF type that allows auto-approval;
  • no crane / cherrypicker;
  • auto-approval enabled for the customer, the primary contractor, the site and the chosen structure / accommodation.

All other SAFs are saved as Pending and reviewed by Towercom. Towercom either approves the SAF or sets it to Feedback Issued with a comment explaining what to change.

Viewing a SAF

An existing SAF opens in view mode:

  • SAF info: ID, author, submitted on, start, end, site, type, status, customer, primary contractor, PICW, access flags, structure / accommodation and summary of works.
  • Crew / Visitors and Equipment Actions tables.
  • Completion Photos and Completion Photos Log (SRF works only).
  • Contractor Files: the H&S and method statement documents attached.
  • SAF Logs: status changes and comments.
  • Existing SAFs: the site's other SAFs.
  • On the right, the Conditions of Access and the booked Time Blocks.

A SAF whose dates have passed shows "SAF is in the past".

Changing, re-using or cancelling a SAF

Editing

Customers and contractors can click Edit while the SAF is Pending, Approved or Feedback Issued, and the SAF isn't in the past. When you re-submit you must describe the changes ("Please describe the changes made to this SAF").

Editing an Approved SAF sends it back to Pending for approval, unless it qualifies for auto-approval. Don't edit an approved SAF just before the visit unless you have to.

Re-using

Click Re Use (or re-use in the list) to start a new SAF from an existing one. You can pick a different Site. The PICW, crew and equipment actions are not copied. Use this for repeat visits, and to replace cancelled or past SAFs.

Cancelling

Click Cancel SAF and enter the reason. Customers and contractors can cancel their own SAFs while they are Pending or Approved. A cancelled SAF can't be reopened; use Re Use instead. Equipment actions on a cancelled SAF are released so they can go on another SAF.

Completion photos

For installation, removal and swap works, Towercom needs photos of the completed works before it can mark the equipment as installed or removed.

  1. After the last day of the SAF (plus a few days' grace), the SAF moves to Awaiting Photos and the primary contractor receives an email asking for photos.
  2. Open the SAF and click Upload Completion Photos.
  3. Click Upload Photo for each photo and add a note if useful.
  4. Click OK. The SAF moves to Photos Received.
  5. Towercom reviews the photos. If all the works are confirmed, the SAF becomes Complete; if only part is confirmed, Partially Complete. The review comment is shown on the SAF.

When the SAF is Partially Complete, you can upload more photos for the remaining works.

For Towercom staff

Staff can create, edit and manage any open SAF.

Changing status

Change Status / Comment sets the status and adds a comment to the SAF Logs. A comment is mandatory for Feedback Issued (it is the feedback to the submitter) and Cancelled. Complete and Cancelled SAFs can't be changed.

Staff can set Photo Req. Delay (days) on SRF-works SAFs: the number of days after the last time block before photos are requested. If left empty, the default applies.

Reviewing completion photos

  1. Assign Reviewer: choose the Completion photo reviewer.
  2. The reviewer clicks Review Photos, enters a Review Comment and ticks the actions that are complete. The review goes for approval (badge Review pending approval).
  3. An approver clicks Approve Photos Review. They can amend the comment before it is issued to the submitter. Alternatively, Send Back to Reviewer with a note (badge Review sent back).
  4. On approval, the ticked actions are completed and the equipment is marked Installed or Removed. The SAF becomes Complete or Partially Complete.

Detaching actions

Staff with more than view permission see a detach link on pending equipment actions, which frees them for another SAF.

Logs

Customers and contractors only see status changes, comments and edits in the SAF Logs. Reviewer assignments, reviews, approvals and detach notes are visible to staff only.
