Finding tickets

Open Trouble Tickets from the menu. Filters: Site, Status, Category, Priority, Raised By, Assigned To, Counties and location. The newest tickets are listed first.

Click the Trouble Ticket ID to open a ticket, or the Site to open the site. A New link on each row starts a new ticket for that row's site.

Customers see the tickets raised for their company. Contractors see tickets for the customers they work for (raised by their own company if they are a contractor only).

Raising a ticket

Start a ticket from:

  • the [T.T.] link on a map pin,
  • the New Trouble Ticket button on the Site page, or
  • the New link in the ticket list.

The form is titled Create New Trouble Ticket or Provide Safety / Security Feedback:

FieldNotes
StatusSet by Towercom. New tickets start as New.
SubmittedFilled in automatically.
CategorySet by Towercom when the ticket is assessed.
Logged byDefaults to you.
Raising CompanyDefaults to your company.
Raised on BehalfThe customer you are raising the ticket for, if any.
Report Issue / Works RequiredMandatory. Describe the problem: what, where on the site, since when, any safety risk.

Add photos (see below), then click Submit and confirm.

If there is an immediate danger to people, call Towercom as well as raising a ticket.

You can edit your ticket while it is still New. Once Towercom has assessed it, it is read-only for you.

Adding photos

In the Open Ticket Files panel, click ADD FILE:

  • File: a .jpg photo.
  • Name (mandatory, filled in from the file name) and Type (mandatory).
  • Description: what the photo shows.

Files are saved when you submit or save the ticket.

What happens next

  • Each ticket opens a support case at Towercom, which the support team works from.
  • Towercom assesses the ticket: sets the category and priority and assigns it to a person (sometimes at a contractor company). The status becomes Assessed.
  • When the issue is fixed, Towercom sets the ticket to Resolved and may add resolution photos.
  • Open the ticket at any time to see its status. Tickets on the same site are listed in Existing Trouble Tickets.

For Towercom staff

Staff see two extra panels and two extra buttons on every ticket.

Assessment

  • Category, Priority, Assigned To Company, Assigned To, Work Required.
  • Assessed is filled with today's date the first time you set any of these.
  • Saving a New ticket with an Assigned To person moves it to Assessed.

Resolution

  • Planned Resolution Date, Resolution Completion Date, CA Taken incl Root Cause.
  • Upload Resolution Photos (when Assessed or Resolved): add .jpg, .jpeg or .png photos with notes. They appear under Resolution Files.

The support case

Open Support Case opens the linked NetSuite case in a new tab. Ticket and case stay in step:

  • Ticket status and category are copied to the case status and issue (and back) through the mapping set up in NetSuite.
  • Changes to Report Issue / Works Required are added to the case as a new message.
  • Planned and completion dates entered on the case are copied to the ticket.
  • The ticket's files are listed on the case's Interactions tab (Twc Files).

Conversations with the customer take place on the support case, not on the ticket. Tickets can also be created automatically from "Support Case" jobs submitted in the FSA mobile app.
