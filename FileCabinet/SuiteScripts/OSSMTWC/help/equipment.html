Site Equipment

See the equipment installed on Towercom sites, and its install and licence status.

Site Equipment is view only. Equipment is added, removed or swapped through a Space Request (SRF) and the works carried out under a Site Access request (SAF). It is never edited on this page.

Equipment classes

ClassMeaning
TMETower Mounted Equipment: antennas, dishes and other equipment mounted on the structure.
ATMEAdditional Tower Mounted Equipment: equipment attached to an existing TME (for example a radio unit behind an antenna).
GIEGround & Indoor Equipment: cabinets and other equipment on the ground or inside the accommodation.
FeedersThe cables connecting TME / ATME to the GIE.

Searching all equipment

Open Site Equipment from the menu (available to customers and Towercom staff). The page is a locator.

Filters

  • Site, Equipment ID, Status (install status), Class, Customer, Counties
  • Hide Inactive (not licenced and not installed) (on by default)
  • Filter by Location: Latitude, Longitude, Radius (Km)

When filters are set, the map only shows the sites that have matching equipment.

The list

Each row shows the equipment ID (with the (i) details icon), structure, customer, install status, licence status, class, type, description, make / model, dimensions (L/W/D), height on tower, the SRF it came from, the site and the infrastructure.

  • Click the SRF to open the Space Request.
  • Click the Site to open the Site page.
  • Click view to open the site's equipment page with that item highlighted.

Equipment on one site

Open it from [Eq.] on the map, from view in the equipment list, or from the Equipment section of the Site page. The site panel is on the left and the equipment tables on the right.

Options in the top bar

  • Customer (staff only): show only one customer's equipment. In the grouped view a TME of another customer stays when some of its ATME or feeders belong to the chosen customer.
  • Hide Inactive (on by default): hide equipment that is neither licenced nor installed.
  • Group (on by default): group the tables by TME.

Grouped view

  • TME / Tower Mounted Equipment: click + next to a TME to see its ATME and feeders, or the + in the header to expand all.
  • GIE / Ground & Indoor Equipment.
  • Unlinked ATME / Feeders: only shown when some ATME or feeders aren't linked to a TME.

Ungrouped view

One table per class. In the ATME and Feeders tables, click the TME value to jump to the parent TME row, which flashes briefly.

Columns

Eq. ID, Class, Install Status, License Status, Customer, Infrastructure, Equipment Type / Feeder Type, Make / Model, Desc, Modified Antenna Location, GIE Location, L/W/D, Height on Tower (m), Az (azimuth), B-End, Cust. Ref, Equipment Flag, SRF.

When you arrive from a view link, the selected item scrolls into view and pulses red for a few seconds.

When the page is opened for a site, staff get an Add Equipment button. It opens a new equipment record in NetSuite in a new tab. Staff also get a link from each Eq. ID to the NetSuite record.

Install and licence status

Each piece of equipment has two statuses: whether it is physically on the site (install status) and whether it is covered by a licence (licence status).

Install status

StatusMeaning
DraftRequested on an SRF that hasn't been submitted.
Not InstalledPlanned but not yet installed.
InstalledOn the site.
RemovedTaken off the site.
CancelledThe request was cancelled. Cancelled equipment is never shown.

Licence status

StatusMeaning
DraftOn an SRF that hasn't been submitted.
Req to LicenceRequested on a submitted SRF.
Prop to LicenceThe SRF was approved; the licence is being prepared.
LicencedCovered by an executed licence (SDS).
Req to Unlicence / Prop to UnlicenceRemoval requested / approved.
UnlicencedNo longer licenced.
Licence RetainedEquipment removed but the licence is kept (package licences).
Licence SwappedReplaced by new equipment through a swap.
CancelledThe request was cancelled.

"Inactive" equipment is equipment that is both unlicenced (Unlicenced, Licence Swapped or Cancelled) and not on site (Not Installed, Removed or Cancelled).
